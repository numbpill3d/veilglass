About veilglass

veilglass lists the Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi devices this machine can hear. It only listens: it never connects to, probes or transmits at anything.

Type is a guess, and each device shows the evidence behind it. Distance comes from signal strength alone, so it is given as a range; walls, bodies and antenna orientation can push the truth outside it. One antenna cannot tell direction, so nothing here shows bearing.

Phones rotate their Bluetooth address about every 15 minutes, so the same phone can leave the list and come back as a new entry. Wi-Fi signal comes from NetworkManager as a percentage; dBm shown for it is converted from that.
